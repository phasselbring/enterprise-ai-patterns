## Result checklist

- [ ] A real result exists, not merely a plan
- [ ] Each material claim has relevant evidence, or is marked unverified
- [ ] Any external actions were explicitly approved and independently authorized
- [ ] Rejected/canceled predecessors were not treated as completed
- [ ] A human reviewer inspected consequential outputs
- [ ] Actual cost and human review time were recorded
- [ ] Acceptance or rejection was logged by the authorized reviewer

Suggested review outcome: **ACCEPT**, **REVISE**, **REJECT**, or **ON HOLD**. These are decisions to document, not automatic actions.
